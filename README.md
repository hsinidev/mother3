Understanding Mother 3


The Mother series, developed by Level-5 and published by Nintendo, is a beloved action role-playing game franchise created in the 1990s. Mother 3, released for the Super Famicom in Japan in 2006, is considered one of the best games in the series.



Game Overview


Mother 3 follows the story of Shigesato Itoi, a Japanese novelist and artist, as he attempts to prevent his son from being killed by an alien invasion. The game is set on the fictional planet of Mother, where humans live in fear of the alien threat.
